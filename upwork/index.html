About me

I'm a Data Scientist who builds the full picture, from the first raw table to the model that makes the call. I design data architecture, automate the reporting nobody wants to do by hand, and build machine learning models that forecast, classify, and predict with real business stakes behind them.

Since 2020, I've worked across e-commerce, real estate, and prediction markets, often as the person setting things up from zero. I work mainly in Python and SQL, and I care about more than accuracy: the best model is the one people trust enough to act on.

I trained as an actuary, so I think in probabilities and risk. I care about how sure a model is, not just what it predicts, which is why my models come with calibrated probabilities and honest uncertainty ranges.
